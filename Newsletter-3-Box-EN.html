What awaits you
in the box

Every box is the beginning of a new adventure – with stories to read aloud, little riddles and a wooden model you build together.

The personalised faux-leather folder
Your child's name is embossed on the folder. All eight chapters of the journey gather here, one by one.
The treasure map
A lovingly illustrated A2 treasure map accompanies the adventure. After every solved chapter a new sticker is added – until the path to the hidden treasure is complete.
The story chapter
A finely printed A5 chapter in which your child becomes part of the story and lives the next adventure alongside Popcorn & Freddy.
The wooden kit
A new task waits in every chapter. Only with the right wooden model can Popcorn & Freddy solve the next riddle and continue their journey.
Brush & paints
Before the adventure goes on, the model you built is painted and given its very own look.
